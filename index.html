Five camera moves, one causal chain

    How to read the colors

    • Cyan primary pathway: water in, protons across the membrane, hydrogen out.
    • Gold energy or actuation: DC current, heat, the pump and control valves.
    • Violet sensing or state change: instrument tags and reaction sites.
    • Neutral oxygen, the by-product, kept out of the accent system.

    These are explanatory overlays, not literal emission. Hardware is drawn solid with hatched cut faces, fields are translucent with dashed contours, and equations sit in boxed grids so the three never blur.

    Accuracy guardrails

    • Generic PEM stack. Not a specific commercial product or a certified design.
    • No efficiency, output or performance claims. The only number shown on screen is the DOE operating-temperature range, attributed.
    • Half-reactions follow the U.S. DOE description: anode 2H₂O → O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻, cathode 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ → 2H₂.
    • Changed scale, slow motion and time compression are labeled on screen whenever they apply.
    • Sectional views only. The removed half is shown as a ghost outline; nothing is disassembled.

    Deterministic by construction

    Every frame is a pure function of its index: no clocks, no stored state, no unseeded randomness. Scrub, step or deep-link to any frame with ?frame=420 and it renders identically every time.
